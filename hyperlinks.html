cool ass sites


www.wendycarlos.com/colorvis/color.html

interesting deep dive into color and our perception of it by wendy carlos. she's also got great pages on other stuff besides her music work, like solar eclipses and maps to name a few.



justsolve.archiveteam.org/wiki/File_Formats

'perpetually under construction' wiki that lists all types of file formats, how they work, what they do, &c. whether it's physical, electronic, or even organic (very interesting section i recommend checking out), it's probably gonna be listed. there is also a button for a random format which is fun too!



npshistory.com

electronic history and archive for national park services. i found a paper on banana slugs somewhere on there. can't find it now but no matter, it's still an interesting place to look around and learn about national parks and their ecosystems



www.angelfire.com/mn/marion/

site where you can learn about ireland's maritime history and development of fishing in the 18th-19th centuries.. pretty cool.



www.hermetic.ch/cal_stud/cal_art.html

this website houses a ton of windows software with articles about them along with manuals. this specific link goes on to talk about the adoption of the gregorian calender, astronomical years, and more.
